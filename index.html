Facilitator Guide

Purpose

Frequency is a prompt-based authentic relating tool designed to warm up expression, reduce social armor, and create genuine moments of connection. Works as an opening activity, mid-session shift, or standalone group experience.

How to Run It

  • Choose a depth together as a group, or let individuals choose
  • Pick a mode: Card (one at a time), Spinner (random wheel), Dice (roll for it), or Spread (5-card tarot layout)
  • One person draws/spins/rolls and reads the prompt aloud
  • They respond briefly or at length, whatever feels right
  • Others can respond to the same prompt or move to the next
  • Pass is always available, no explanation needed

Depth Guide

  • Noticing — What you observe. Best for new groups or warming up. Low stakes, high connection.
  • Sharing — What you're actually feeling. For groups with some trust. Invites real feelings without requiring deep vulnerability.
  • Risking — What's hard to say out loud. Reserve for groups with strong cohesion. Model vulnerability yourself first.

Trauma-Informed Reminders

  • Pass is always valid — name it at the start and model it yourself if needed
  • No processing required after sharing — just receiving is enough
  • Witnessing silently is full participation
  • Watch for dysregulation at Risking depth — slow down, return to Noticing if needed
  • Closing grounding after Risking is recommended

Integration Prompts

  • What was it like to hear someone else's answer?
  • Was there a moment you felt less alone?
  • What made it easier or harder to share?
  • What do you want to carry out of this room today?
